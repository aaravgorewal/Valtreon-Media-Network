## Executive Mandate & Anti-Card Philosophy

This document defines the complete **Co-Production Capabilities & Services Section** for the Valtreon Media Network (VMN) platform.

In strict adherence to VMN's permanent **Digital Hygiene Directive** and our commitment to **Swiss Modernist Design**, we explicitly reject "simple cards"—the ubiquitous, low-effort AI template clichés (e.g., 3 identical white boxes with top-centered icons, short bullet points, and "Learn More" links). Simple cards fail enterprise buyers because they commoditize high-value offerings into generic agency menu items.

Instead, VMN structures its services as an **Interactive Architectural Co-Production Console**. The architecture features a **Master Capabilities Accordion & Telemetry Split-Panel System** (Columns 1–5 for capability selection and narrative storytelling; Columns 6–12 for real-time live telemetry, 4K showreel previews, campaign architecture diagrams, and direct scope intake gates).
