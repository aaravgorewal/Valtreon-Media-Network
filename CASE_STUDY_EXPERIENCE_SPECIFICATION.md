## Executive Mandate & Anti-Boring Card Philosophy

This specification defines the complete **Case Study & Campaign Dossier Experience Architecture** for the Valtreon Media Network (VMN) platform.

In strict adherence to VMN's permanent **Digital Hygiene Directive** and our commitment to **Swiss Modernist Design**, we explicitly reject "boring project cards"—the low-effort, AI-generated clichés that plague agency portfolios (such as a 300px rounded image, a generic project title like "Brand Redesign", a 2-sentence summary, and a colored "View Case Study" tag). Boring cards treat enterprise achievements as superficial web trinkets, failing to convince sophisticated buyers.

Instead, VMN structures its case studies as **Audited Enterprise Campaign Dossiers**. Each featured campaign is presented as an architectural masterwork comprising 4K cinematic film stills, real-time performance telemetry, vector comparison charts, verified CFO/CMO audit receipts, executive testimonials, and direct co-production scope blueprints.

### 5.2 Styling & Presentation Rules
- **Anti-Gloss Rule**: No colorful brand logos or glowing badges. All logos are rendered in desaturated, high-contrast monochrome Zinc (`#A1A1AA` / 50% opacity), brightening to Alabaster White (`#F4F4F5`) on hover.

# 6. SCREENSHOTS & HIGH-FIDELITY CINEMATIC ARTIFACTS

### 6.1 Artifact Presentation Guidelines
- **Cinematic Framing**: Screenshots of film scenes, digital dossiers, or interactive whitepapers are framed inside 1px Zinc architectural viewports with dark matte overlays (`bg-zinc-950`).
- **35mm Film Grain Overlay**: A subtle 2% noise grain is applied to video stills to maintain a high-art cinematic quality.
- **Micro-Interactive Hover**: Hovering over any artifact image initiates a smooth 60fps video showreel loop (`muted playsInline loop`) highlighting actual film footage or dossier interactions.

---

# 7. MOTION ARCHITECTURE & INTERACTIVE ANIMATIONS

### 7.1 Kinetic Transition Sequence
1. **Dossier Mount**: When scrolled into view, the dossier frame enters with a subtle 12px Y-axis slide up and opacity fade over 300ms (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
2. **Chart Line Drawing**: The SVG pipeline trajectory line draws itself across the chart canvas over 800ms using CSS `stroke-dashoffset`.
3. **Tabular Count-Up**: Telemetry numbers count up smoothly from 0 to target values using tabular monospace digits (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
